Our fair play promise

Same game for everyone

Difficulty depends only on your score or level. Never on your device, country, or whether you watch ads.

No rigged losses

We never make you lose to show you an ad, and we never change your score behind your back.

Ads you choose

Rewarded ads are always marked, and "No thanks" is just as big. Close an ad early and nothing is taken from you.

Your data stays put

No accounts and no tracking of our own. Scores and saved games stay on your phone.
