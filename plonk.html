Plonk! Block Puzzle

A bright, relaxing block puzzle you can play in a minute or for an hour.

Get it on Google Play Plonk! help

How to play

Drag pieces onto the 8x8 board. Fill a row or a column to clear it. Clear lines back to back to build a combo and score big, and empty the whole board for a Spotless bonus. The run ends when no piece fits.

Ads in Plonk!

  • When you run out of room, you can watch an ad to clear a strip of the board and keep going, once per run.
  • "New pieces" swaps your tray for an ad, twice per run.
  • After your second game, an ad may play between games.

The pieces you get depend only on your score. Ads never change them.
