Star Lab Games privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Star Lab Games is the games label of Star Lab Systems, United Kingdom ("we"). This policy covers every game we publish, on Google Play (Android) and on the web (our own pages, itch.io, CrazyGames, GameDistribution and sites that embed GameDistribution games). Our games include Plonk! Block Puzzle and Orbit Hop. Where a game works differently, this policy says so.

What our games store

Our games save your scores, recent runs, game progress, extra lives and settings (such as sound and vibration) on your device only. We do not run servers, accounts or analytics of our own, and we do not receive this information.

Leaderboards (Android, Google Play Games)

Games with a leaderboard use Google Play Games Services. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your scores are sent to Google so they can appear on that game's leaderboard, together with your Play Games gamer name and avatar. The game shows the top players and your own rank, which it reads from Google. Google handles this data under its own privacy policy. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile in the Play Games app or your Google Account.

Ads

Our Android games show ads from Google AdMob. Some ads are optional: you choose to watch one for a reward such as an extra life or to continue a game. Others play at natural breaks, such as reaching a new level or between games. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, information about how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, our apps ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the game's Privacy button (on the home screen or in the menu). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Web versions on game portals (such as CrazyGames or GameDistribution) show ads through that portal, under the portal's own privacy policy. Our own web pages may show Google ads once approved.

Ads never change a game. Difficulty depends only on your score and is the same for every player.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Clearing an app's storage, clearing your browser's site data, or uninstalling the app deletes everything the game has saved. Leaderboard scores are deleted with your Play Games profile.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: game.support@starlabsystems.uk

If you are in the UK, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
